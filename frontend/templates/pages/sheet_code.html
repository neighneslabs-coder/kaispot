Letters that never appear in a word the sheet already spells have to be named once, below. After that they are remembered for every sheet you read.

Or paste the list from Mikhmon

Open the same voucher batch in Mikhmon, select the list of usernames and passwords on the page, copy it, and paste it in the box. The pairs are matched to card 1, card 2, card 3 and so on, in the order they are printed — so paste the whole batch from the top.

It copes with tabs, commas, pipes or spaces between the two, and ignores heading words like Username and Password. Nothing else about a card changes: its number, its price and whether it has been sold all stay as they are.
